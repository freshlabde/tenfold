Four ways to draw the same ten goals

The same vault in every panel: ten ranked goals, three of them broken down, one of those two levels deep. Rank one is always the largest and the brightest. Judge each one by the three questions the map actually has to answer — which goals are still bare, how far each has got, and where am I.

Recommendation

Keep the constellation, fix it. It is the only screen in tenfold that is not a list; replacing it with columns or a tree would duplicate the outline and buy nothing. Its faults are execution faults, not concept faults: a horizontal axis that carries no meaning, families that are all one metal, labels cut mid-word and dropped on top of orbs, and numerals too small to read below rank five. All four are fixable without touching the idea.

What changes: a ten-hue data palette carries family identity (rank keeps carrying itself through size and light), labels wrap to two lines and are pushed clear of every disc, the bodies grow so the sky reads as fuller, and a bottom veil holds one line that says the thing is tappable.
